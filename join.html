Ready to Join the Adventure?

Pack 149 welcomes youth in Kindergarten through 5th grade. Start with your Pack 149 Registration and it will walk you the rest of the way.

How to Join

How Joining Works

Pack 149 Registration

This is where everyone starts, new and returning. Your Pack 149 Registration tells our leadership your scout is joining, adds you to pack communications, and places your scout in the right den. Registering more than one child? Please complete it once for each scout.

Start Your Registration ›

Scouting America Registration

As soon as you submit your Pack 149 Registration, it links you straight to the Scouting America Registration. Complete that and your scout is officially registered.

Your Scout Is Official

Once your Pack 149 Registration and your Scouting America Registration are both complete, your scout is a fully registered member of Pack 149 and ready for their first meeting.

Have Questions?

We're happy to answer any questions about the pack, dues, uniforms, or what to expect at your first meeting.
